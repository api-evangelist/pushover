Today I’m happy to announce the release of Pushover 3.7 for Android , iPhone/iPad , and Desktop with custom sound support! While Pushover has long included 21 different sounds that can be selected per-message through our API, a common feature request was the ability to play custom notification sounds stored on a user’s device. This was technically possible on Android but there was no ability to do it on iPhone/iPad, and on Android there was no way to choose between different sounds on a user’s device through the API.
